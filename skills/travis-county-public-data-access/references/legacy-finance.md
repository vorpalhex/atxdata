# Legacy Campaign Finance — WILL NOT IMPLEMENT

The [legacy county campaign-finance search](https://www2.traviscountytx.gov/county_clerk/election/finance/finance_search.asp) exists. County guidance advertises filings from January 1, 1997 through November 11, 2021; modern EasyVote is separate.

The search index worked, but every published PDF link we attempted returned HTTP 404, including ordinary browser clicks. A ten-link date-spread sample tested on November 5, 2026 covered source-listed filing dates from June 17, 1997 through November 4, 2021: **0/10 originals available (0% availability yield)**. Earlier individual attempts also returned 404. These are filing dates, not PDF creation dates. This sample does not prove every archive document is unavailable or that any required report was not filed.

Operator decision: **WILL NOT IMPLEMENT**. No collector or working original-document acquisition is provided. Do not retry, expand discovery, contact the clerk or resume implementation unless the operator explicitly reopens this system. Retained private evidence is historical provenance, not an active work queue.
